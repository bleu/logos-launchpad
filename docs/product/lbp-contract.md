# Fixed-price and weighted sale mechanics

The fixed-price sale has a four-transaction setup path, public exact-input and exact-output buys, permissionless fee collection, creator proceeds settlement and a separate creator-allocation claim on the actor interface. Fixed-price and weighted sales also support optional graduation under [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-settlement-and-graduation.md). See [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-fixed-price-lbp-setup.md), [ADR 0033](../adr/0033-fixed-price-lbp-recorded-collateral.md) and the [workspace guide](../architecture/workspace-guide.md#execution-and-evidence-limits) for local signed execution checks and limits. Real proofs and deployment readiness remain pending.

## Fixed-price setup

The shared launch factory can create a new launch token, set positive sale inventory and an optional creator allocation, bind the sale to the curve-owned canonical `NamespaceConfig`, and validate setup. Creation saves an immutable `AfterSetup` or `AfterEnd` allocation release choice. The pool starts with zero recorded collateral. The price is a positive exact ratio in smallest units; token decimals are display data. The start uses the inclusion-time bounds defined in [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-fixed-price-lbp-setup.md). Creation selects `Scheduled`, `Depletion` or `Either`. `Scheduled` and `Either` require an end; setup and activation must finish before it. `Depletion` has no end deadline. Only selected depletion closes early; `Scheduled` waits for the end even if inventory is empty. The per-sale creator role initializes with the creator signer in the creation claim. Final setup funds sale inventory into the fixed-price sale vault. `AfterSetup` pays the creator allocation only after successful final setup; `AfterEnd` keeps it in escrow until the selected ending policy makes it eligible. Its separate claim follows [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-sale-closing.md). Unsolicited collateral remains surplus and does not block activation or enter sale accounting. See [ADR 0033](../adr/0033-fixed-price-lbp-recorded-collateral.md). [ADR 0032](../adr/0032-fixed-price-lbp-setup.md) defines setup rules and review limits.

## Settlement

A fixed-price sale ends under its selected policy without a final trade or separate close call. Selected depletion is final; burns during settlement do not create depletion evidence. Only the current creator-role holder can authorize manual payouts, and that creator is the recipient. Manual proceeds settlement pays all recorded net proceeds, collects remaining fees to the current namespace treasury and burns unsold sale inventory. Donations remain surplus. The separate `AfterEnd` allocation claim can run before or after proceeds settlement. Successful retries do not pay or burn twice. See [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-sale-closing.md).

## Weighted setup and trading

The factory creates weighted sales in the same four setup stages. Typed weighted inputs set a positive collateral seed, normalized weight endpoints, and an explicit start and end. Final setup transfers the full seed from the creator. Weighted public trades read one pricing timestamp through a temporary interface. Local tests supply a fake clock. The application has not integrated a supported timestamp-reply provider, so normal builds reject weighted trades. Sale inclusion bounds still enforce the scheduled start and end. See [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-lbp-clock-and-poke.md) for clock rules, [ADR 0030](../adr/0030-lbp-pricing-collateral.md) for seed and collateral accounting and [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-shared-launch-factory.md) for shared policies.

Weighted controls and settlement are implemented. Their selected-ending rules and payout checks are in [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-sale-closing.md). Local guest evidence is in the [workspace guide](../architecture/workspace-guide.md#execution-and-evidence-limits).

## Scope

The implemented scope includes creation, funding, public trades, permissionless fee collection and creator payouts. Trade modes and sell rules follow [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-modes-and-open-limits.md); quote and provider-time limits follow [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-quotes-and-slippage.md). Retained creator fees remain future work. Trader-borne fixed-price fees are an additional RFP-016 departure with external status `To be reviewed by Logos team`. Delayed settlement, cancellation and expired-recovery handlers remain future work. Public buy allowlists are implemented under [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-modes-and-open-limits.md); see [signed integration evidence](../../plans/allowlist-program-integration.md#public-execution-evidence). Wallet allowlist flows, weighted virtual accounting, private fixed-price or weighted sales, and full SDK and CLI flows remain out of scope.
